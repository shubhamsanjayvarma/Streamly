Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does the direct bank transfer work?

When a viewer sends a tip, the payment is routed directly to your personal Amazon Pay or Merchant UPI ID. The money never sits in Streamly escrow—it goes straight to your bank account in real-time.

Do I need a business account or GST to use Streamly?

No! With our Amazon Pay integration, you can connect your normal personal Amazon Pay UPI account without requiring any merchant documentation or business registration.

What are alert tokens?

Since 100% of the tip money goes directly to your bank, Streamly cannot deduct fees from the payment itself. Instead, you keep a small prepaid balance of Alert Tokens (1 Token = ₹1). When a tip occurs, a 5% or 7% token fee is deducted to fuel the automated alerts and OBS widgets.

Is there any monthly subscription?

Never. There are zero monthly charges, zero setup costs, and zero hidden platform maintenance fees. You only pay for the tips you actually receive.
